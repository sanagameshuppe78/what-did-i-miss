How "What Did I Miss?" Solves Group Chat Overload

Never waste 20 minutes scrolling through hundreds of unread messages. Our local deterministic intelligence engine identifies actionable tasks, deadlines, decisions, and your personal mentions in milliseconds.

Direct & Alias Mentions

Highlights requests directed at you (e.g. @Alex, Alex please review), distinguishing actual action requests from casual name drops.

Action Items & Assignments

Identifies commitments and requests, associates responsible people, and provides an interactive checklist with completion states.

Decisions vs Contradictions

Separates agreed resolutions from unresolved debates and actively warns you when chat members contradict earlier statements (e.g. room shifts).

Privacy & Local Execution Guarantee

100% Client-Side Local Execution

Zero bytes of conversation content ever leave your browser.

How Your Privacy Is Protected:

  • No Cloud AI APIs: Summaries, tasks, and deadlines are extracted entirely using in-browser deterministic regular expressions, entity recognition, and sentence scoring algorithms in vanilla JavaScript.
  • Zero Network Requests: The app does not transmit chat text, sender names, or extracted metadata to any server, database, or analytics platform.
  • No Persistent Chat Logging by Default: Your raw conversations are stored only in volatile memory during the active session.
  • One-Click Data Purge: Clicking "Clear Data" immediately wipes all parsed state and cache from memory.
  • Open & Inspectable: You can open browser Developer Tools (F12) > Network tab to verify that no network requests occur when analyzing conversations.
Engine Specification: Local Rule-Based NLP Pipeline v1.2 (Extractive Lexical Ranker + Temporal Entity Matcher + Sentence Intent Classifier).
